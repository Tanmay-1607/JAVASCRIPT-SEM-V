About Smart India Hackathon

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a national-level initiative that encourages students to develop innovative solutions to real-world problems.
It promotes creativity, teamwork, and technical skills.

Achievements

Students participating in hackathons gain practical experience, improve their problem-solving abilities, and develop innovative projects.
Innovation leads to progress!

Career Opportunities

Participation in hackathons helps students strengthen their technical portfolios and demonstrate their skills to potential employers.
Learn, collaborate, and build your future.
